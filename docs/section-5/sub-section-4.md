# Verify your organisation

If you're a registered Kenyan organisation, you need to verify your account before receiving government data. Verification uses the KRA iTax system and your Business Registration Number (BRN).

On the organisation dashboard, click **Verify Now**.

![Unverified organisation](/img/verificationGuide/unverified.png)

## Add your Business Registration Number (BRN)

Enter your organisation's Business Registration Number and click **Submit**.

![Add BRN](/img/verificationGuide/AddBrn.png)

## Verification result

### If the BRN is invalid

You will see a failure screen. Click **Retry** to correct your BRN and try again.

![Verification failed](/img/verificationGuide/failed.png)

### If the BRN is valid

Your BRN is accepted and the system proceeds to the next step.

![BRN verified](/img/verificationGuide/verify.png)

## Confirm with OTP

An OTP is sent to the registered company registrar. Enter the OTP on the confirmation page. **You have 15 minutes to complete this step.**

![Confirm OTP](/img/verificationGuide/confirmotp.png)

## Verification complete

Once the OTP is accepted, your organisation thumbnail updates to show a verified badge.

![Verified organisation thumbnail](/img/verificationGuide/verifiedthumbnail.png)

You can now confirm your organisation and access all verified features.

![Verified organisation](/img/verificationGuide/verifiiedorg.png)
