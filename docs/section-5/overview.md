# Overview

## Actions

To access the TLIP platform simply go to https://app.tlip.io

To access the TLIP API simply contact the team and
Request for testing credentials

The following actions can be performed in the TLIP platform

#### [Sign Up](./sub-section-1)

Create a new TLIP account using your email address.

#### [Create your organization](./sub-section-2)

Set up your organisation profile with a name and local details.

#### [Add users to your organization](./sub-section-3)

Invite team members to collaborate within your organisation.

#### [Verify your organisation](./sub-section-4)

Confirm your registration via Business Registration Number (BRN) and OTP to unlock access to government data.

#### [Log In](./sub-section-5)

Access the platform with your credentials.

#### [Reset Password](./sub-section-6)

Recover access to your account if you have forgotten your password.

#### [Consignments Overview](./sub-section-7)

View and manage all your active consignments in one place.

#### [Add an identifier to a consignment](./sub-section-9)

Attach reference numbers such as commercial invoice, UCR, or container numbers to a consignment.

#### [Add a document to a consignment](./sub-section-10)

Upload and manage trade documents in the consignment's e-Pouch.

#### [Share a consignment on TLIP](./sub-section-11)

Grant another TLIP organisation access to a consignment and choose which documents they can view.

#### [Share a document outside of TLIP](./sub-section-12)

Send a consignment document to anyone via email or a shareable link, no TLIP account required.
