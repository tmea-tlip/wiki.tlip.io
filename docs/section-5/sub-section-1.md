# Sign Up

Create a new TLIP account in a few steps.

## Go to the sign up page

Click **Sign Up** on the login page.

![Sign up button on the login page](/img/userGuide/su1.png)

## Enter your details

Enter the required details and click **Sign Up**.

![Sign up form](/img/userGuide/su2.png)

## Verify your email

Click the **Verify** button in the email received from notifications@tlip.io.

- Make sure to check your spam folder if you can't find the email in your inbox.

![Email verification message](/img/userGuide/su3.png)
