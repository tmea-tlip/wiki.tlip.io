# Share a document outside of TLIP

You can share individual consignment documents with people outside of TLIP via email or a shareable link.

Open a document in the e-Pouch and click **Share document**.

![Share document](/img/share/sharedocument.png)

## Share by email

Enter one or more email addresses (separated by commas) in the **Email** field and click **Share**.

## Share via link

Click **Copy link** to copy a public link to the document that anyone can open.
