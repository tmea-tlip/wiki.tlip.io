# Share a consignment on TLIP

You can share a consignment with another organisation on TLIP, giving them access to its documents.

On the consignment details page, click **Share consignment**.

![Share consignment](/img/share/shareconsingment.png)

Enter the name of the organisation you want to share with and click **Next**.

## Choose document access

Select whether the organisation can access all documents in the consignment or only specific ones.

### Access all documents

Select **Access all documents** and click **Share**.

![Share all documents](/img/share/sharealldocs.png)

### Access specific documents

Select **Access specific documents**, then search for and select the documents you want to share. Click **Share** to confirm.

![Choose specific documents](/img/share/choosespecificdocs.png)
